One simple routine

Start with 10–15 minutes each day.

01
Learn a few key points, not a whole speech.

Use the three clue words on each answer. Similar questions share a response pattern.

02
Close the answer and speak from memory.

Trying to retrieve information is more powerful than only rereading it.

03
Come back later and mix up topics.

Review hard questions again; practise unexpected questions from different categories.
